Pricing

Everything instructional on this site is free to read. The pose library, all four sequences, the guides and every reference page. No account, no email address, no paywall on any of it.

That is paid for by display advertising. If you would rather not see ads, or you want the material in a form you can print and take to a mat, there is an optional membership. Nothing is removed from the free version to make the paid one look better.

How this site is funded

Six revenue sources, all disclosed. If any of them ever influences what we recommend, we will say so on the page it affects.

  • Display advertising. The main source. Ads are labeled, appear below the start of the content, never touch buttons or navigation, and never appear on legal pages or the error page.
  • Membership. 8.00 US dollars a month or 72.00 US dollars a year. Removes ads and adds downloads, saved collections and printable cards.
  • Affiliate commissions. On mats, blocks, straps and bolsters. Disclosed next to the link every time it appears, not only in the footer. We list household substitutes for every prop because buying is optional.
  • Paid digital products. The three items above, priced in US dollars, one-time purchases.
  • Sponsored content. Occasionally a brand may pay for an article. If that happens the word Sponsored appears at the top of the page above the headline and the sponsor is named. We have none published at present.
  • Teacher directory. A basic listing is free for any teacher. A featured placement is paid, and featured listings are marked as featured wherever they appear.

The full disclosure, in detail

What membership will not do

  • It will not unlock instructional content. Nothing that teaches a pose or a sequence sits behind the paywall, now or later. If we publish a new pose guide, it goes in the free library.
  • It will not include personal instruction. We are not a teaching service and we do not review your practice. For that, work with a qualified teacher in person. Our directory is free to use.
  • It will not include health guidance. No part of this site, paid or free, offers medical advice or individual health recommendations.
  • It will not auto-renew silently. Renewal reminders will be sent by email seven days in advance of every charge, monthly or annual.

Billing questions

For memberships, a full refund within 14 days of the first charge, no questions asked. For digital products, a full refund within 14 days of purchase. Request either by emailing hello@firstlightyoga.com. Full terms are in the terms of use.

Yes, all prices are in US dollars. Applicable state sales tax will be calculated at checkout based on your billing address. If you are outside the United States your card issuer may apply a currency conversion fee that we do not control and do not receive.
