Practices

Sequences

Four practices, from eight minutes at a desk to a half hour on the floor. Each one lists its total duration, difficulty, the props you need and every pose in order with a hold time, and each has a timer built into the page so you can keep your eyes off the clock.

  • 10 minutes
  • Beginner
  • No props required

10-Minute Morning

Fourteen holds that move from a folded rest through standing shapes and back to the floor. Designed to be done before the day starts, in whatever you slept in, without a mat if there is carpet under you.

Open the sequence
  • 8 minutes
  • Beginner
  • One sturdy chair

Desk Reset

Twelve holds done entirely in a chair, without changing clothes or getting on the floor. Twists, side bends, hip shapes and a chair version of Warrior Two, for people whose days are spent seated.

Open the sequence
  • 15 minutes
  • Beginner
  • Blanket, bolster or pillows, wall

Evening Wind-Down

Ten long, supported holds, mostly seated and on the back, finishing with three minutes of legs up a wall. Nothing in it requires strength, and every shape has something under it.

Open the sequence
  • 30 minutes
  • Beginner to intermediate
  • Mat, two blocks, strap, blanket

Gentle Full Practice

Twenty-eight holds covering a full arc: warm-up on the floor, standing shapes, a balance, floor back bending, and eight minutes of resting shapes at the end. The one to grow into.

Open the sequence

How to use the timer

Every sequence page carries a player. Press start and it counts down the first hold, announces the next pose and moves on by itself. You can pause at any point, skip forward or back, or click any pose in the list to jump to it.

The list underneath the player is the sequence in plain text, with names, Sanskrit and hold times. It reads perfectly well without the timer, and it prints as a clean one-page practice sheet if you would rather have paper on the floor beside you than a screen.

Holds are suggestions. If a shape needs longer, pause. If it needs less, skip on. The order matters more than the seconds.

Choosing between them

  • Short on time and standing: 10-Minute Morning.
  • At a desk, in work clothes: Desk Reset.
  • Late in the day, low energy: Evening Wind-Down.
  • You have half an hour and want a whole practice: Gentle Full Practice.
  • Brand new to all of it: read Start Here first, then do the 10-Minute Morning three times before adding anything.

Before you practice any sequence

These sequences are general movement education, not medical advice. Talk with a qualified healthcare professional before beginning a new physical practice, particularly if you are pregnant, recently injured, post-surgical, or managing a health condition. Stop if you feel pain, and work with a qualified teacher in person for hands-on guidance.
