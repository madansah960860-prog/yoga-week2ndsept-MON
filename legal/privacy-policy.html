Privacy policy

This policy explains what First Light Yoga LLC collects when you use this website, why, who else is involved, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it.

Last updated 09/01/2025. Effective 09/01/2025.

The short version

  • You can read every instructional page on this site without giving us anything.
  • We do not set analytics or advertising cookies until you choose to allow them.
  • We do not sell your personal information for money.
  • Advertising on this site may involve sharing certain identifiers with advertising partners including Google, which counts as sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law. You can turn that off.
  • The site is not for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
  • You can change your cookie choices at any time using the cookie preferences link in the footer of every page.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact form. Your name, email address, the topic you selected and the message you wrote. Used to reply to you.
  • Newsletter signup. Your email address, and your first name if you choose to give it. Used to send the newsletter you asked for.
  • Teacher directory submissions. The information you submit for your listing, which is published on the directory page because that is its purpose.
  • Membership and purchases, when available. Your name, email address and billing address. Card details are handled by our payment processor and never reach our servers.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs. Your IP address, the pages requested, timestamps, referring page, and your browser and operating system version. Collected by our hosting provider for security and reliability. This happens for every website you visit and is necessary to serve you a page.
  • Cookie choice. A record of the choice you made in the cookie banner, stored in your browser so we do not ask again. This is strictly necessary and set regardless of your choice.
  • Analytics, only with your consent. Page views, approximate location at city level, device type and referrer, in aggregate.
  • Advertising, only with your consent. Identifiers used by advertising partners to select and measure ads. See section 4.

What we do not collect

We do not collect health information, we do not ask about medical conditions, and we do not want you to send us any. We do not build profiles of individual readers. We do not use fingerprinting techniques to identify you when you decline cookies.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small file stored by your browser. This site uses three categories.

  • Strictly necessary. Always active. Includes the record of your cookie choice and anything needed for security and basic delivery of pages. These cannot be switched off because the site cannot work without them.
  • Analytics. Off until you allow them. Used to count how many people read which pages so we know what to write more of.
  • Advertising. Off until you allow them. Used by advertising partners including Google to select ads, cap how often you see the same one, and measure whether ads worked.

No non-essential cookies are set before you make a choice. When you first arrive, the banner appears and nothing beyond the strictly necessary category is written until you press a button on it.

You can change your mind at any time using the Cookie preferences link in the footer of every page. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, which will not stop you reading anything here.

For a full description of each cookie category, see the cookie policy.

4. Advertising, and Google's use of cookies

This site is free to read and is funded partly by display advertising. If you consent to advertising cookies, the following applies.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by Google by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising from many participating vendors at optout.aboutads.info, run by the Digital Advertising Alliance, and at optout.networkadvertising.org, run by the Network Advertising Initiative.
  • Third-party vendors other than Google may also serve ads on this site and may use their own cookies. Their use of information is governed by their own privacy policies.
  • Declining advertising cookies does not remove advertising from the site. It means the ads you see are not selected based on your browsing history.

We do not give advertisers your email address, your name, or the content of anything you send us.

8. California residents: CCPA and CPRA

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act gives you specific rights.

Categories of personal information

In the last 12 months we may have collected: identifiers such as IP address and email address; internet or network activity such as pages viewed and referring site; approximate geolocation at city level; and commercial information such as purchase records. We collect these from you directly and automatically from your device, for the purposes described in section 2.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

We do not sell personal information for money. However, allowing third-party advertising cookies may constitute sharing personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law, and you have the right to opt out of that.

To opt out, do either of these:

  • Use the Cookie preferences link in the footer of any page, turn Advertising off, and save. That is the fastest route and takes effect immediately in this browser.
  • Email hello@firstlightyoga.com with the subject line Do Not Sell or Share, or write to us at the address in section 1.

We honor Global Privacy Control signals. If your browser or extension sends a GPC signal, we treat it as a valid opt-out request for that browser without you needing to do anything else.

Your other California rights

  • Right to know what personal information we have collected, used and disclosed about you, and to receive a copy.
  • Right to delete personal information we hold about you, subject to legal exceptions such as tax records.
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by the CPRA.
  • Right not to be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights. Nothing on this site becomes unavailable, slower or more expensive because you opted out.

To make a request, email us with the subject line California privacy request. We will verify your identity by asking you to confirm information we already hold, and we will respond within 45 days, extendable once by a further 45 days where necessary. You may use an authorized agent, and we may ask for proof of their authorization.

9. Other US state privacy laws

Residents of states with comprehensive privacy laws, including Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon and Montana, have broadly similar rights to access, correct, delete and obtain a copy of their personal information, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Use the same routes described in section 8 and tell us which state you reside in. Where a state provides a right of appeal against our decision, you may appeal by replying to our response and we will reconsider within the period that state's law requires.

10. Visitors from the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom

This site is intended for readers in the United States, but it is reachable from anywhere and some readers are in Europe. If the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to you, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your personal information, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing already carried out.

Our servers and several of our service providers are located in the United States, so using this site involves transferring your information there. Where required, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, or an equivalent mechanism, as the legal basis for that transfer.

To exercise any right, email hello@firstlightyoga.com. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority, or to the Information Commissioner's Office in the United Kingdom.

11. Children's privacy

This site is intended for adults. It is not directed at children, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 years of age, and we do not serve advertising targeted at children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, email hello@firstlightyoga.com and we will delete it promptly. This is consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA.

12. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to the small amount of personal information we hold is limited to people who need it, and payment card details are handled entirely by our payment processor and never stored by us. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected users and the relevant authorities without undue delay if a breach affecting personal information occurs.

13. Links to other sites

This site links to other websites, including retailers through affiliate links and teacher listings in the directory. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of any other site. When you follow a link away from here, that site's own privacy policy applies.

14. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we handle your information, we will say so in the newsletter and, where the change concerns cookies, ask for your consent again.
