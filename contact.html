Where to send specific things

  • Privacy and data requests, including access, deletion and Do Not Sell or Share requests: use the form above with the topic set to privacy, or email us with the word privacy in the subject line. Details of what we do with those requests are in the privacy policy.
  • Accessibility problems: tell us the page, what you were using and what happened. We treat these as bugs, not feedback. See the accessibility statement.
  • Advertising and sponsorship: the work with us page has our rates, formats and standards. Use the form with the advertising topic.
  • Teacher directory listings: free listings and featured placement are both handled through the form. Include your city, state, the styles you teach and any credential you want shown.
  • Corrections: the most useful message you can send. Page name and the sentence is plenty.

What we cannot answer

We cannot give medical advice, assess an injury, tell you whether a pose is safe for your specific condition, or offer individual health guidance. Those questions belong with a qualified healthcare professional who can examine you and knows your history, and we will say so if you ask.

We also cannot assess your practice from a written description or a photograph. For that, work with a qualified teacher in person. Our directory is free to use.
