Newsletter

One short letter, twice a month

Two emails a month, on the first and third Tuesday. Each one carries a new pose breakdown, one sequence, and a plain note about anything that changed on the site. That is the whole letter.

What is in each issue

  • One pose, broken down. The same nine-part structure as the library pages: names, difficulty, entry steps, cues, breath, hold, common mistakes, modifications and contraindications.
  • One short sequence. Usually five to fifteen minutes, with hold times, and always with a chair or wall version.
  • What changed. New pages, corrections we made, and anything we got wrong and fixed. Corrections go at the top, not the bottom.

What is not in it

  • No daily mail. Two a month, and we do not send extra ones because we felt like it.
  • No countdowns, no fake deadlines, no last chance subject lines.
  • No health claims. We do not tell you a pose will fix anything.
  • No weight loss, calorie or body transformation content, ever.
  • No selling in the first line. If there is something for sale it is at the bottom and labeled.

Email terms, stated plainly

United States email law, the CAN-SPAM Act, requires a few things of anyone sending commercial email. Here they are, before you sign up rather than buried in a footer.

  • Who is sending it. Every email comes from First Light Yoga LLC. The sender name will read First Light Yoga and the reply address will be a real monitored inbox, hello@firstlightyoga.com, not a no-reply address.
  • Honest subject lines. The subject line will describe what is in the email. It will not imply a reply, a transaction or an urgent matter that does not exist.
  • A physical mailing address. Every email carries our postal address: First Light Yoga LLC, 1412 Pearl Street, Suite 204, Boulder, CO 80302, United States.
  • A working unsubscribe link. In every email, in the footer, one click, no login, no survey, no are you sure. Requests are honored promptly and in any case within 10 business days, as the law requires.
  • Double opt-in. After you submit the form you will receive one confirmation email. You are not subscribed until you click the link in it. If you never click, you hear nothing further.
  • We do not sell or rent the list. Your email address is not shared with advertisers, sponsors or any other third party for their own marketing.

How we handle your data is set out in the privacy policy, including retention periods and how to ask for your data to be deleted.
