Partnerships

Work with us

Four ways to work with First Light Yoga: display advertising, sponsored content, freelance writing, and paid featured placement in the teacher directory. Rates, formats and the standards attached to each are below, before you have to email anybody.

What we will not accept, in any format

This list is not negotiable and applies equally to display advertising, sponsored content and directory listings.

  • Any claim that a product, pose, sequence or breathing practice treats, cures, heals, relieves or prevents a medical condition.
  • Weight-loss framing of any kind: calorie or fat-burning claims, before-and-after imagery, body-transformation or slimming language, body-shape commentary.
  • Diet products, supplements, cleanses, fasting programs, detox products or teas.
  • Teacher training or certification advertised with earnings claims or guaranteed outcomes.
  • Anything targeting people under 18.
  • Fake scarcity, countdown timers, invented statistics or testimonials the advertiser cannot substantiate.
  • Creative that imitates editorial content, imitates a system alert, or is designed to be mistaken for part of the site.

Display advertising

Ad slots are sold through programmatic partners and, for a small number of positions, directly. Every slot on this site follows the same rules regardless of who fills it.

  • Labeled. Every slot carries a visible Advertisement label.
  • Placed below the content. No ad sits above the start of the main content of a page.
  • Separated. Ads never touch navigation, buttons or form controls.
  • Space reserved. Slot dimensions are reserved in the layout so nothing shifts as an ad loads.
  • Density capped. A maximum of two slots on a standard page and three on the longest guides, always well below the content density.
  • Never on legal pages or the error page. No exceptions.
  • No interstitials, pop-ups, pop-unders, auto-playing audio or forced redirects. Not now, not for a premium.

Available formats. 970 by 250 and 728 by 90 on desktop, 320 by 100 and 300 by 250 on mobile, and 300 by 600 in the sidebar on wide viewports.

Direct rates. Available on request. Direct placements are sold by month, not by impression, and a media kit with current traffic figures is sent on request through the contact form.

Sponsored content

We accept a small number of sponsored articles. The terms are the same for everyone and are not adjustable.

  • Labeled at the top. The word Sponsored appears at the very top of the page, above the headline, before a reader has read a word of the article. The sponsor is named in the same label.
  • Written by us. We write the piece. You brief us, you check factual accuracy about your own product, and you do not have copy approval over the editorial judgment.
  • Held to the same standards. Every rule on the rest of this site applies: no health claims, no weight-loss framing, no invented testimonials, no unsupportable superlatives.
  • Honest. If we do not think a product is good, we will not write about it, and we will refund rather than write something we do not believe.
  • Nofollow links. All sponsor links are marked as sponsored links for search engines, as Google requires.
  • Permanent labeling. The label stays on the page for as long as the page exists. We do not quietly remove it later.

Here is exactly what the label looks like at the top of a sponsored page:

Sponsored. This article is paid for by an advertiser and is labeled as sponsored content. Example label only, no sponsor is named because no sponsored article is currently published. Read our advertising and affiliate disclosure.

We have no sponsored articles published at this time.

Writing for us

We commission a small amount of freelance writing, mostly pose guides and sequence pages. We are looking for writers who can be precise about movement without being mystical about it, and who will happily write the chair version at the same length as the floor version.

What we pay. 350.00 US dollars for a complete pose guide, and 500.00 US dollars for a sequence page including the full pose-by-pose ordering with hold times. Paid within 14 days of publication, in US dollars, by direct deposit or check.

What a pose guide must contain. English and Sanskrit names with correct diacritics and a pronunciation, a difficulty judgment with reasoning, numbered entry steps in the order a body actually moves, alignment cues, a breath pattern, a suggested hold range, common mistakes with fixes, at least two accessible modifications using a chair, wall, block or strap, at least one progression, and a contraindication note naming who should approach the shape with care.

What we will not commission. Anything making therapeutic claims, anything with weight-loss or body-shape framing, listicles, and anything written with a large language model and submitted as original work.

To pitch. Use the contact form with the writing topic. Send one paragraph on the pose or sequence you want to write, one paragraph on your practice and teaching background, and one previous piece of writing. We reply to every pitch.

Teacher directory placement

Free listing. Any yoga teacher or studio in the United States can be listed at no cost. Includes your name or business name, city and state, styles taught, a short description in your own words and one link.

Featured placement. 25.00 US dollars a month or 250.00 US dollars a year, in US dollars. A featured listing appears at the top of its state, carries a longer description, a photograph and up to three links, and is labeled Featured listing, paid wherever it appears.

What paying does not buy. It does not change how we describe you, does not exempt your listing from our content standards, and is not an endorsement. We do not verify credentials for free or featured listings and we say so on the directory page.

Affiliate partnerships

We participate in affiliate programs for mats, blocks, straps, bolsters and blankets. If you run one and would like to be considered, get in touch.

How we handle them: disclosure appears next to the link at the point of use on every page, not only in the footer. We recommend the same products whether or not a link pays. We list household substitutes for every prop. And we do not accept affiliate arrangements for anything on the excluded list above.
