Advertising and affiliate disclosure

This site makes money in six ways. All six are listed here, and every one of them is also disclosed on the page where it appears rather than only in this footer link. That is the standard the Federal Trade Commission expects, and it is also just the decent way to do it.

Last updated 09/01/2025.

The disclosure, in one paragraph

First Light Yoga is free to read and is funded by display advertising. Some links to yoga props on this site are affiliate links, which means that if you buy something after following one, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. We also sell an optional membership and downloadable products, we may publish clearly labeled sponsored articles, and teachers can pay for featured placement in our directory. None of this changes what we recommend, and household substitutes are listed for every prop because buying anything is optional.

1. Display advertising

Display advertising is the main source of revenue and is what pays for the free pose library, the sequences and the guides.

  • Every ad slot carries a visible Advertisement label.
  • No ad sits above the start of the main content of a page.
  • Ads never touch navigation, buttons or form controls.
  • Slot dimensions are reserved in the layout so nothing shifts as an ad loads.
  • A maximum of two slots on a standard page and three on the longest guides.
  • No ads at all on legal pages or the error page.
  • No interstitials, pop-ups, pop-unders, auto-playing audio or forced redirects, at any price.

Advertising cookies are only set with your consent. See the cookie policy and the privacy policy, including how to opt out of personalized advertising by Google.

We do not control which specific advertisements appear through programmatic partners. If you see an ad on this site that breaks the standards on our work with us page, particularly a health, weight-loss or supplement ad, please tell us and we will block that category or advertiser.

2. Affiliate links

Some links to yoga props on this site are affiliate links. If you follow one and buy something, the retailer pays us a small commission. The price you pay is exactly the same. You are not charged extra, and no discount is withheld from you.

Where they appear

Affiliate links appear in the props and modifications guide, in the props article, and occasionally in a pose page where a specific prop is central to a modification. They do not appear in the pose library index, the sequences, the legal pages or the newsletter's editorial section.

How they are disclosed

Every page that contains an affiliate link carries a disclosure on that page, near the link, in normal-sized readable text rather than a footnote. The wording is some version of this:

Affiliate disclosure: this page contains affiliate links. If you buy through one we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. It does not change what we recommend.

What commission does not buy

  • It does not change which props we recommend or in what order.
  • It does not stop us listing household substitutes. Every prop on this site has one, including the ones we link to commercially, because a rolled blanket genuinely does the job for most people starting out.
  • It does not stop us saying a product is not worth buying. If we would not use it, we do not link it.
  • It does not create any relationship between you and us if you buy. Your transaction is with the retailer, and any warranty, return or dispute is between you and them.

Programs we participate in

We participate in affiliate programs covering yoga mats, blocks, straps, bolsters and blankets. The specific programs and any required program-specific disclosure language will be named here before launch, as each program's terms require.

3. Membership

An optional membership at 8.00 US dollars a month or 72.00 US dollars a year removes display advertising and adds downloadable sequence PDFs, saved practice collections, printable pose cards and a practice log.

No instructional content is behind it. Every pose guide, every sequence and every reference page is free and stays free. Membership buys convenience and an ad-free reading experience, not access to teaching. Full billing terms are in the terms of use, and pricing is on the pricing page.

4. Paid digital products

We sell downloadable products: a four-week beginner program, a bundle of twelve printable sequences, and a printable pose card set. Prices are in US dollars and are listed on the pricing page. These are one-time purchases with no subscription, and a full refund is available within 14 days.

5. Sponsored content

We may accept payment from a brand to publish an article. When we do, all of the following apply.

  • The word Sponsored appears at the very top of the page, above the headline, before you read a word of the article.
  • The sponsor is named in the same label.
  • We write the article. The sponsor briefs us and checks factual accuracy about their own product, and has no approval over the editorial judgment.
  • Every standard on the rest of this site applies: no health claims, no weight-loss framing, no invented testimonials.
  • All sponsor links are marked as sponsored links for search engines.
  • The label stays on the page permanently. We do not quietly remove it later.

Here is exactly what the label looks like:

Sponsored. This article is paid for by an advertiser and is labeled as sponsored content. Example label only. No sponsor is named because no sponsored article is currently published.

We have no sponsored articles published at this time.

6. Teacher directory placement

A basic listing in the teacher directory is free for any yoga teacher or studio in the United States. A featured listing, which appears at the top of its state with a longer description and a photograph, costs 25.00 US dollars a month or 250.00 US dollars a year.

Every featured listing is labeled Featured listing, paid wherever it appears. We do not verify credentials for free or paid listings and we say so on the directory page. Paying does not buy an endorsement, and it does not exempt a listing from our content standards.

Editorial independence

No advertiser, affiliate partner or sponsor sees an article before it publishes, has any right of approval over it, or has any influence on which props we recommend or how we describe them.

If a commercial relationship ever affects what appears on a page, we will state it on that page. If you think something on this site reads like it was written for a commercial reason rather than an editorial one, tell us. That is a serious complaint and we will treat it as one.

What we refuse, regardless of price

  • Any advertisement or sponsored piece claiming a product, pose, sequence or breathing practice treats, cures, heals or prevents a medical condition.
  • Weight-loss, fat-burning, slimming, before-and-after or body-transformation advertising.
  • Supplements, diet products, cleanses, fasting programs and detox products.
  • Anything targeting people under 18.
  • Creative designed to look like editorial content or a system alert.
  • Fake scarcity, countdown timers, invented statistics and testimonials the advertiser cannot substantiate.
