Nobody needs to be flexible to begin

First Light Yoga is a reading and reference site. Every pose page tells you how to enter the shape, what to feel for, how to breathe, how long to stay, and what to do instead if the full version does not suit your body today.

There are no classes to buy before you can read anything, no login wall on the library, and no assumption that you can already reach your toes. Chair, wall and prop-supported versions sit beside every pose as equal options, not as a lesser tier.

Written for real bodies

Most instructional yoga writing assumes a body that goes to the floor easily, holds weight on the wrists without complaint and has no history worth mentioning. That is a narrow assumption, and it leaves out a lot of people who would like to practice.

On this site, a chair version of a standing pose is written with the same care as the floor version. A wall gives you a reliable reference for balance. Blocks bring the ground closer. A strap extends your reach. None of that is a downgrade. It is how the shape gets built for the body in front of it.

How this site is funded

Short version: display ads pay for the free library, and a few optional things are for sale. Nothing here is hidden.

  • Display advertising. The pose library, the sequences and the guides are free to read and paid for by ads. Ad slots are labeled and kept away from buttons and navigation.
  • Optional membership, 8.00 US dollars a month or 72.00 US dollars a year. Ad-free browsing, downloadable sequence PDFs, saved practice collections and printable pose cards. The free library does not shrink when you do not pay.
  • Affiliate links. When we link to a mat, block, strap or bolster we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. That is disclosed on the page, next to the link, every time.
  • Paid digital products. Downloadable beginner programs and sequence bundles, priced in US dollars, listed on the membership page.
  • Sponsored content. Occasionally a brand pays for an article. When that happens the label sits at the very top of the page before you read a word of it.
  • Teacher directory. A basic listing is free for any teacher. A featured placement is paid.

Before you practice

Everything on First Light Yoga is general movement education. It is not medical advice, and it is not a substitute for care from a qualified healthcare professional. If you are pregnant, recently injured, recovering from surgery or managing a health condition, talk with a professional who knows your history before starting a new physical practice.

Work at a level where you can breathe evenly and speak a sentence. If something hurts, come out of it. For hands-on guidance, practice with a qualified teacher in person.
