While you are here

Everything on this site is general movement education. It is not medical advice. Speak with a qualified healthcare professional before beginning a new physical practice, particularly if you are pregnant, recently injured, post-surgical or managing a health condition. Stop if you feel pain, and work with a qualified teacher in person for hands-on guidance.
