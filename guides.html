How we write these

Every guide is written by a person, checked against the pose pages it links to, and dated so you can see how current it is. When we update one materially we change the date and say what changed at the bottom.

We do not publish anything that claims a pose, a sequence or a breathing pattern treats, prevents or improves a medical condition. Where a guide describes what a shape does, it describes it physically: which joints move, which muscles lengthen or work.

We do not publish weight-loss content, calorie figures, body-transformation framing, cleanses, fasting or detox material of any kind, and we do not intend to.

Advertising and sponsorship

These guides are free to read and are supported by display advertising. Ad slots are labeled, sit below the start of the article, and never sit between you and a button.

Some guides contain affiliate links to props. Where they do, the disclosure appears in the article next to the link, not only in the footer. If you buy through one we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

If an article is paid for by a brand, the label Sponsored appears at the very top of the page before the headline, and the sponsor is named. We have no sponsored guides published at this time, and any that appear will carry that label.
