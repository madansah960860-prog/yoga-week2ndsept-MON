Cookie policy

This page explains what cookies this site uses, what each category does, and how to change your mind at any point. It sits alongside the privacy policy, which covers everything else about data.

Last updated 09/01/2025.

Change your choices now

You can open the cookie preferences panel at any time, from this page or from the footer of every page on the site.

Your choice is stored in this browser only. If you use a different browser or device, or clear your browsing data, you will be asked again.

What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store. It can hold a value that the site reads back later, such as a setting you chose or an identifier used to recognize a browser across visits. Some are set by this site directly, and some by other companies whose services appear on a page. The same rules here apply to similar technologies such as local storage and pixels.

Consent comes first

Nothing beyond the strictly necessary category is set before you make a choice. When you first arrive, the banner appears, and until you press a button on it we set only the one item required to remember what you decide.

The banner gives you three options: Accept all, Reject non-essential, and Manage preferences, where you can allow analytics without allowing advertising, or the other way round. Rejecting is exactly as easy as accepting, in one click, and nothing on the site becomes unavailable if you reject.

2. Analytics

Off until you allow them.

Analytics cookies let us count how many people read a page, roughly where in the world they are at city level, what device they used, and which site sent them here. We use this to decide what to write next. It is aggregate information; we do not use it to build a profile of you or to identify you.

3. Advertising

Off until you allow them.

Advertising cookies are set by third-party vendors, including Google, and are used to select which ads to show, to limit how many times you see the same one, and to measure whether an ad led to anything.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by Google at Google Ads Settings.
  • You may opt out of many participating vendors at optout.aboutads.info and optout.networkadvertising.org.
  • Turning these off does not remove advertising from the site. It means the ads are not chosen based on your browsing history.

Third-party content on this page

Two things load from other domains on every page and are worth naming.

  • Google Fonts. The typefaces used across the site are requested from Google's font servers. That request includes your IP address, as any request to any server does. No cookie is set by it.
  • Photographs. Images are served from a third-party image host. The same applies: your IP address is part of the request, and no cookie is set.

Controlling cookies in your browser

Independently of anything on this site, you can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Doing so will not stop you reading anything here.

  • Chrome. Settings, then Privacy and security, then Third-party cookies.
  • Safari. Settings, then Privacy.
  • Firefox. Settings, then Privacy and Security.
  • Edge. Settings, then Cookies and site permissions.

We also honor Global Privacy Control. If your browser or an extension sends a GPC signal, we treat it as an opt-out of advertising cookies and of sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising, without you having to do anything else.

What we do not do

  • We do not set non-essential cookies before you consent.
  • We do not use browser fingerprinting or any similar technique to identify you when you decline cookies.
  • We do not make rejecting harder than accepting.
  • We do not treat continued scrolling as consent.
  • We do not restrict content based on your cookie choice.

Changes and questions

If we add a vendor or a new category, we update this page and ask for your consent again. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
