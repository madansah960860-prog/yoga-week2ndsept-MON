About First Light Yoga

First Light Yoga is a small independent publisher of instructional yoga writing, based in Boulder, Colorado. We write pose guides, sequences and reference material for people practicing at home in the United States, and we give it away.

Why the site exists

Most instructional yoga writing on the internet assumes a body that goes to the floor easily, holds weight on the wrists without complaint and has no history worth mentioning. It also tends to promise things it cannot deliver, usually about health conditions, and often about how your body should look.

We wanted a reference that did neither. Every pose here is written with chair, wall and prop-supported versions given the same care and the same word count as the floor version, because those are not lesser options, they are how a shape gets built for the body in front of it. And every description of what a pose does is a physical description: which joints move, which muscles lengthen or work. Not what it supposedly fixes.

What we will never publish

  • Any claim that a pose, sequence or breathing pattern treats, cures, heals, relieves or prevents a medical condition.
  • Weight-loss framing of any kind: calorie figures, fat-burning language, before-and-after content, body-shape commentary.
  • Diet content, cleanses, fasting or detox material.
  • Invented testimonials, invented reviews or star ratings we did not collect.
  • Credentials we cannot substantiate.

The short version

  • What it is. A free, ad-supported instructional reference for home yoga practice.
  • Who it is for. Adults in the United States who want to practice at home and would like the writing to take their actual body into account.
  • What it is not. A medical resource, a clinical service, a class platform or a fitness program.
  • Who runs it. First Light Yoga LLC, a limited liability company registered in Colorado, operating from 1412 Pearl Street, Suite 204, Boulder, CO 80302.
  • Founded. 2021.

How the content is made

  1. Written by a person. Each pose page is drafted from practice and from published anatomical and pedagogical sources, then rewritten until the entry steps match the order a body actually moves in.
  2. Checked against the structure. Every pose page must carry all nine parts: names with diacritics, difficulty, entry steps, alignment cues, breath pattern, hold time, common mistakes, at least two modifications, at least one progression and a contraindication note. A page missing any of them does not publish.
  3. Read for claims. A separate pass removes any sentence that implies a therapeutic effect, any weight or body-shape language, and any instruction that would be unsafe without supervision.
  4. Cross-linked. Poses link to the sequences they appear in, sequences link to the poses they use, and every one of them links to the health and safety disclaimer.
  5. Dated and corrected in public. Pages carry a published date and an updated date. When we get something wrong we fix it and say so in the newsletter, at the top rather than the bottom.

If you think something here is wrong, tell us. Corrections are the most useful email we get. Contact us.

How this site is funded

Plainly, and in one place. If a revenue source ever influences what we recommend, we will say so on the page it affects.

  • Display advertising. The main source of revenue. It pays for the free library. Ad slots are labeled, kept below the start of the content, and never placed on legal pages or the error page.
  • Optional membership. 8.00 US dollars a month or 72.00 US dollars a year, for ad-free browsing, downloadable sequence PDFs, saved practice collections and printable pose cards. No instructional content is removed from the free tier to support it.
  • Affiliate commissions. On mats, blocks, straps and bolsters. Disclosed next to every link, at the point of use, not only in the footer.
  • Paid digital products. Downloadable beginner programs and sequence bundles, priced in US dollars.
  • Sponsored content. If a brand pays for an article, the word Sponsored appears at the top of the page above the headline and the sponsor is named. None is published at present.
  • Teacher directory. Basic listings are free for any teacher. Featured placement is paid at 25.00 US dollars a month, and featured listings are labeled wherever they appear.

Editorial independence

No advertiser, sponsor or affiliate partner sees an article before it publishes, has any right of approval over it, or has any influence on which props we recommend. A sponsor buys a labeled placement, not editorial control.

We recommend household substitutes for every prop on the site, including the ones we link to commercially, because the honest answer for most people starting out is that a rolled blanket does the job.

Accessibility

The site is built to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Semantic landmarks, a skip link, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, alt text on every image, contrast checked against the background, and motion that respects the reduced motion setting in your operating system. If something here does not work for you, tell us and we will fix it. Accessibility statement.
